How Escrogen Works

Four steps from locked funds to trust-minimized settlement.

01
🔒

Create Escrow

The buyer locks GEN and writes plain-language deliverable requirements. Funds are held by the contract — nobody can move them unilaterally.

02
📤

Delivery & Verification

The seller submits a public evidence URL. It is stripped of scripts/tags and wrapped in <UNTRUSTED> fences before the AI ever reads it.

03
🧠

AI Dispute Resolution

GenLayer's multimodal LLM consensus inspects the live evidence against the rules — validators re-run independently and agree only on the canonical verdict.

04
⚡

Instant Settlement

Based on consensus, funds release to the seller, refund gross to the buyer, or split — settled on-chain automatically, no human arbiter.

Canonical Verdicts

RELEASE_TO_SELLER

Deliverable clearly meets the requirements.

REFUND_TO_BUYER

Missing, failed, or unusable — buyer refunded in full.

SPLIT_FEE_50_50

Partial or genuinely ambiguous delivery.

🛡️ Off-enum or malformed AI output raises [LLM_ERROR], forcing validator rotation instead of settling on corrupted results.
